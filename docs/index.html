Every letter is a real character, turned and cut

Each source was found by searching all 6,763 GB2312 characters in a bold Hei font: turn a quarter-turn, then cut straight across, at most half. The score compares the cut piece with the Tangping letter (1.00 is identical). Y has no clean source; 卡 is the closest. C, E, F, H, K, R and T were redrawn to follow their sources: 冂's hook, 山's long middle arm, 工's short left stroke, 刀's curved leg, 十's wide bar.
