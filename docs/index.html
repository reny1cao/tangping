How it is drawn

Five rules from one character

A

Thick bars, thin stems

Bars are Hei vertical strokes turned sideways, so they carry more weight than the stems.

bar 135 · stem 100
m

Cut at the baseline

Every stem ends in a straight slice, where the character's left edge was cut away.

flat cut · y = 0
N

撇 down, 捺 up

Strokes falling to the right taper like 撇. Strokes rising to the right sweep past the top like 捺.

DS

Curves bend like Hei turns

Where a Latin letter is round, its corners bend the way 九, 己 and 乚 bend. O stays a square 口.

turn radius 130

Two heights

Capitals stand tall. Lowercase stands short, as M and N do in the original, with ascenders and descenders.
