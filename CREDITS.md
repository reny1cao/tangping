# Credits

## Original idea

**サイバー環状線 — [@CyberKanjousen](https://x.com/CyberKanjousen) on X**

On 21 October 2026 they posted:

> 以防你不知道：曼 从中间劈开就是 MAN！
> *(In case you didn't know: split 曼 down the middle and it's MAN!)*

with an illustration, signed *made by X@CyberKanjousen*, showing 曼 → 曼 on its side → mAn.
Tangping takes that observation and asks whether the whole Latin alphabet can be found the same way.
The M, A and N in these fonts follow the shapes in that post; the rest of the alphabet follows rules derived from it.

The illustration itself is not part of this repository and is not redistributed here.
If you write about or share Tangping, please credit the idea to @CyberKanjousen as well.
